Jollof Rice Recipe

Learn how to prepare delicious, smoky Nigerian-style Jollof Rice using this recipe from Ola's Fastfood Restaurant.

Ingredients

  • Rice: Long-grain parboiled rice (or golden sella basmati).
  • Pepper Base: Fresh Roma tomatoes, red bell peppers (Tatashe), Scotch bonnet peppers (Ado/Rodo), and red onions blended smooth.
  • Tomato Concentrate: Concentrated tomato paste for deep color and rich flavor.
  • Stock: Rich beef or chicken stock.
  • Oil: Vegetable or canola oil.
  • Aromatics: Sliced onions, crushed garlic, and minced ginger.
  • Seasoning Cubes: Bouillon cubes such as Maggi or Knorr.
  • Spices: Nigerian curry powder, dried thyme, white pepper, and bay leaves.
  • Salt: Add to taste.
  • Butter: One tablespoon stirred in at the end for a shiny finish.
  • Smoky Finish: Butter and sliced onions charred slightly at the bottom of the pot to create the signature smoky party Jollof flavor.

Cooking Instructions

  1. Blend the Pepper Base: Blend red bell peppers, Scotch bonnet peppers, tomatoes, and one onion until smooth. Set aside.
  2. Fry the Aromatics and Tomato Paste: Heat vegetable oil in a heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Sauté sliced onions for 3–5 minutes until soft. Stir in tomato paste, garlic, and ginger. Fry for 5–7 minutes until the paste darkens and loses its sour taste.
  3. Fry the Pepper Sauce: Pour in the blended pepper mixture. Season with curry powder, dried thyme, bouillon cubes, white pepper, bay leaves, and salt. Cover and simmer for 15–20 minutes until the sauce reduces, the oil floats to the top, and the raw taste disappears.
  4. Prepare and Add the Rice: Rinse the long-grain parboiled rice thoroughly under warm water until the water runs clear. Pour the rinsed rice and hot beef or chicken stock into the pot. Stir once to combine. Ensure the liquid level sits slightly above the rice.
  5. Steam Low and Slow: Cover the pot tightly with aluminum foil and place the lid on top to trap the steam. Turn the heat to very low and cook undisturbed for 25–30 minutes until the rice absorbs the liquid and becomes tender.
  6. Get the Smoky Finish and Serve: Remove the foil. Add a tablespoon of butter and sliced onions on top. Turn the heat to medium-high for 3–5 minutes, allowing the bottom layer to char slightly. Remove from heat, fluff with a fork, and let it sit covered for 5 minutes before serving.
  7. Enjoy! Serve your delicious smoky Jollof Rice.
Ola's Tip: The secret to smoky Jollof Rice is allowing the bottom of the rice to lightly char while cooking. Don't burn it — you're looking for that pleasant smoky aroma and flavor.
